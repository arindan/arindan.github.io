Arindan Mandal

Assistant Professor, Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad

Thank you for visiting my webpage.

I am part of the Department of Climate Change, where my research focuses on the cryosphere (snow, glaciers, permafrost, etc.) and climate.

Our current research particularly focuses on:

  • snow-glacier energy and mass balance
  • sublimation of snow
  • mountain hydro-meteorology
  • permafrost degradation
